# Legal & Compliance

This is a living project checklist, not legal advice. Current requirements must be re-checked against the official Apple, Google and EU sources before release.

## App stores

- [ ] Apple Developer account / agreements
- [ ] Apple App Review requirements checked
- [ ] Google Play developer account / agreements
- [ ] Google Play policies checked
- [ ] Age/content rating completed
- [ ] Store metadata and screenshots comply with platform rules

## Privacy / GDPR

- [ ] Data inventory created
- [ ] Lawful basis / purpose documented where applicable
- [ ] Privacy policy drafted
- [ ] In-app privacy information available
- [ ] Data retention considered
- [ ] Data deletion process defined where required
- [ ] Third-party processors / SDKs documented
- [ ] Consent requirements assessed
- [ ] Apple App Tracking Transparency requirements assessed if tracking is used

## Advertising

- [ ] Ad provider selected
- [ ] Ad provider terms reviewed
- [ ] SDK privacy/data practices reviewed
- [ ] Ad formats comply with Apple/Google policies
- [ ] Age-sensitive advertising requirements assessed
- [ ] Consent / tracking implementation assessed

## In-app purchases

- [ ] Apple IAP requirements reviewed
- [ ] Google Play Billing requirements reviewed
- [ ] Products and pricing defined
- [ ] Restore-purchase flow implemented where applicable
- [ ] Refund / support process defined

## Intellectual property

For every third-party asset, record source, license, permitted commercial use and attribution requirements where applicable.

- [ ] Graphics/assets licensed
- [ ] Fonts licensed
- [ ] Music licensed
- [ ] Sound effects licensed
- [ ] Code dependencies licensed
- [ ] Third-party trademarks avoided or properly used

## Business / consumer requirements

- [ ] Business registration timing assessed
- [ ] Tax/VAT implications assessed
- [ ] Consumer information requirements assessed
- [ ] Terms of service assessed
- [ ] Support/contact information available

## Release gate

No commercial release until the relevant checklist items have been reviewed and the project documentation records the sources and decisions.
