# Game Design

## Working concept

SWING is a mobile, momentum-based game focused on timing, movement and mastery.

### Core loop

1. Identify the next hook.
2. Attach / swing.
3. Build and manage momentum.
4. Release at the right moment.
5. Reach the next hook or obstacle section.
6. Continue the run.
7. Fail, restart and try to improve.

## Design goals

- Easy to understand.
- Difficult to master.
- Short, replayable runs.
- Strong sense of momentum.
- Clear and satisfying feedback.
- Skill should be more important than grinding.

## Current scope

The first proof of concept should contain only the minimum systems required to answer one question:

> Is the core swinging mechanic fun?

No commitment has yet been made on the final art style, final game name, exact camera perspective, progression model or number of worlds.

## Future possibilities

Potential future systems include procedural generation, obstacles, world-specific mechanics, progression, cosmetics, premium content and competitive/high-score systems. These are ideas rather than committed features until validated.
