# Future Ideas

Ideas are collected here without implying that they will be implemented.

- Multiple worlds with different mechanics
- World-specific physics
- Advanced obstacles
- Dynamic music
- Cosmetics
- Challenges / events
- Leaderboards
- Replay / ghost systems
- Social features
- Additional progression systems
- Content updates

Ideas should be moved into the roadmap only after they have a clear reason to exist and the core game supports them.
