# Audio

Audio is part of the core product experience and should be designed into the architecture rather than added as an afterthought.

## Audio layers

### Music

- Menu / ambient music
- Gameplay music
- Potential world-specific themes
- Potential dynamic music based on gameplay intensity

### Sound effects

Potential core effects:

- Hook attach
- Swing start
- Momentum / speed feedback
- Release
- Successful connection
- Near miss
- Obstacle impact
- Death
- New high score
- UI interactions

### Environment

Potential world-specific ambience such as wind, rain or mechanical/environmental sounds.

## Audio principles

- Audio should reinforce timing and momentum.
- Sound effects should provide clear gameplay feedback.
- Music and SFX volumes should be independently adjustable.
- Assets must have documented commercial usage rights.

## Asset licensing

Every final audio asset should have a documented source and license before commercial release.
